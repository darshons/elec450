\textbf{Best observed settings.} Across all settings, the fastest median was RRT-Connect $r=2.5$ (.0189 s), the shortest was RRT-Connect $r=.2$ (4.114 rad), smoothest was PRM $k=10$ (.400), and greatest sampled minimum clearance was RTP $b=.05$ (10.57 cm). Each used 25 exact paths. Excluding paths that failed the finer check changes the shortest median to RRT $b=.05,r=1$ (4.117 rad, 23 paths); the other overall winners stay the same.
