Some queries were harder for RRT than others. At bias .05 and range .2, it solved 2/5, 3/5 and 2/5 trials on q1/q2/q5, but all five on q3 and q4. Bias .2 at range 1 solved only 2/5 on q5. With bias .05 and range 1, only one q5 trial was approximate; range 2.5 solved all 25 trials. The overall success rate hides these differences. The approximate run at bias .05 and range 1 took 5.03806 s, which is included in the timing summaries.

RTP, RRT-Connect and PRM solved all five trials on every query at every setting. Five trials per query are too few to guarantee that these planners will always succeed. All settings used the same queries, but individual trials did not use matching seeds. These results show differences in our tests; they do not establish statistical significance or a ranking that applies to every scene.
